THESEUS COMMANDS

Inside Claude Code, in your Theseus workspace. Plain words work too:
"what's next, I have 20 minutes" is the same as /next.

YOUR LIST
  /next [what's done, time]   the best thing to do now
  /add <anything>             add a task, reminder, event, grocery or chore
  /inbox                      process your Notes inbox
  /rank [now]                 quick A-or-B choices to put things in order
  /refresh                    re-run everything, fresh
  /weekly                     weekly cleanup: archive, stale items, rules

TEXTS
  /texts                      who's waiting on a reply
  /reply <name>               draft a reply in your words (never sent)
  /tone                       refresh your writing style
  /history [count]            is all your message history on this Mac?

UNDERSTANDING A CONVERSATION (only when you ask)
  /learn <name>               deep, neutral notes on how you two talk
  /mirror <name> [focus]      an honest outside read on how you come across
  /report <name> [since]      neutral, timestamped account of a long thread
  /research <topic>           search and compile across your texts

RUN NOW AND OTHER
  /morning                    the morning briefing
  /nightly                    the evening prep
  /sync                       a quiet sync
  /slack [answers]            Slack check-in
  /migrate                    one-time move from Reminders, Things and old notes
  /compliance                 re-read Anthropic's rules against how Theseus works
  /commands                   this list

SETTING UP
  /theseus:setup              the setup interview
  /theseus:doctor             health check
  /theseus:update             update the engine

IN TERMINAL
  theseus                     chat with Theseus
  theseus "a question"        ask one thing and print the answer
  theseus status              your list and what's running (instant)
  theseus next [n]            the top of your list, 5 by default (instant)
  theseus today               your Today note (instant)
  theseus add "text"          add to NEXT, or use --to <section> (instant)
  theseus done "text"         check off what matches (instant)
  theseus undo                take back the last add or done, until the next sync (instant)
  theseus texts               who's waiting on a reply (instant; Terminal needs Full Disk Access)
  theseus wall [show|status]  what your screen shows, and how it's doing (instant)
  theseus wall setup <host>   connect a Raspberry Pi screen, once
  theseus wall titles on|off  show task titles on that screen, or only counts
  theseus wall view <name>    change the view on that screen
  theseus wall hours 6am 12am when that screen is on and when it sleeps
  theseus wall off            clear that screen and stop sending to it
  theseus sync                sync now
  theseus morning             run the morning briefing now
  theseus version             the installed version
  theseus help                this list

Instant commands don't use Claude. Nothing is ever sent.
